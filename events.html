FAQs

What to expect when you attend.

Are events free?

Yes. Donations are welcome, and sliding-scale contributions help fund future programming.

Do you offer childcare?

Family-friendly lounges are available at all in-person events with books, art supplies, and quiet activities.

How do virtual sessions work?

We send a Zoom link and resource packet in advance. Closed captioning and interpretation are available.
