Why we exist

We are a community of psychologists, parents, and youth mentors reimagining mental healthcare.

Project Smile listens first and builds programming with the people we serve. Our mission is to create welcoming paths to therapy, peer connection, and lifelong coping skills for families navigating emotional health.

Mission

Listening deeply, acting collectively.

Project Smile was founded in 2018 after a coalition of school psychologists and caregivers mapped gaps in accessible support. We launched pop-up wellness rooms in community centers, paired families with bilingual therapists, and created a peer mentor network. Those prototypes became the foundation for today’s multi-hub nonprofit.

2018 — Pilot listening sessions

Gathered 150 families to identify barriers to traditional therapy and co-designed early workshops.

2019 — First neighborhood hub

Opened our Brookdale hub with drop-in counseling navigation and caregiver collaboratives.

2021 — Youth mentor studio

Launched peer mentor training rooted in positive psychology and restorative practices.

2023 — Digital learning campus

Introduced a virtual resilience studio offering multilingual mindfulness and coping workshops.

Our approach

Psychology expertise meets community wisdom.

Co-design

Families and youth lead planning sessions that shape program goals, curriculum pacing, and support services.

Continuity of care

We bridge short-term workshops with long-term clinical referrals, ensuring follow-up beyond graduation.

Data with heart

Surveys, storytelling, and qualitative interviews inform improvements and celebrate progress.
