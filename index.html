Our Mission

Founded by a collective of educators and creatives, AIM Youth envisions a world where every young person sees themselves as a catalyst for change. We design immersive experiences that nurture courage, sharpen skills, and celebrate cultural identity.

Belonging

Inclusive cohorts that uplift every story and background.

Ingenuity

Idea incubators that empower teens to dream and build boldly.

Momentum

Strategic partnerships that open doors to college and careers.

Since 2015

We have guided 800+ youth leaders with personalized mentorship, global service trips, and a community that cheers for every milestone.

What we offer

Programs designed to spark purpose

Mentor Circles

  • College and scholarship guidance

Impact Studio

Real-world challenges that teach design thinking, social innovation, and collaborative problem solving.

  • Human-centered design sprints
  • Pitch showcases with local partners

Global Journeys

Cultural immersion trips that deepen empathy, service, and global citizenship.

  • Service-learning travel
  • Language and cultural exchanges
  • Storytelling and documentary labs
