Whole-family psychology care

Project Smile helps young people thrive through culturally responsive mental health support.

Our counselors, peer mentors, and educators guide families through evidence-informed tools that make emotional wellbeing approachable, joyful, and sustainable.

Our promise

We make trusted psychology resources welcoming, inclusive, and actionable.

Project Smile began as a grassroots network of caregivers searching for stigma-free support. Today we partner with community clinics and school-based counselors to offer spaces where children, teens, and adults co-create healing practices rooted in belonging and cultural wisdom.

Belonging first

Every program centers affirmation, multilingual access, and trauma-informed facilitation.

Science meets story

We blend clinical research with lived experience so skills translate into everyday life.

Community momentum

Partnerships with schools, clinics, and cultural leaders expand pathways to long-term care.

Project Smile snapshot

Born in 2018 through a coalition of neighborhood counselors and parents, Project Smile now supports families across three borough hubs and an online learning studio.

What we offer

Holistic mental health programs for young people and the adults who love them.

Therapy navigation

Care coordinators help families understand benefits, match with therapists, and prepare for first sessions.

  • Personalized clinician matching
  • Insurance & sliding-scale planning
  • Warm handoffs to specialty care

Resilience studio

Mindfulness, expressive arts, and body-based practices cultivate emotional literacy and stress relief.

  • Drop-in mindfulness labs
  • Art therapy workshops
  • Movement and breath sessions

Caregiver collaboratives

Monthly cohorts offer coaching on co-regulation, communication, and building supportive home routines.

  • Facilitated peer circles
  • Skill-building toolkits
  • Family action planning

Impact

Evidence-backed care that changes how families access mental health support.

Regular evaluations show Project Smile programs improve emotional literacy, increase follow-through on therapy referrals, and reduce stigma around discussing mental health at home and in classrooms.

0 Families connected to trusted therapists each year
0 Youth reporting stronger coping skills after eight weeks
0 Caregivers who maintain peer support beyond graduation
0 Community partners collaborating on mental wellness events
