Core offerings

Choose the pathway that aligns with your goals.

Therapy navigation

Our care navigators demystify the therapy process. We review benefits, explore culturally responsive options, and prepare families for the first appointment.

  • One-on-one intake and benefits review
  • Curated therapist recommendations
  • Session preparation guides for youth and caregivers

Ideal for

Families beginning therapy, returning after a pause, or seeking specialized support.

Session cadence

Three 60-minute meetings plus follow-up check-ins.

Resilience studio

Experiential workshops use mindfulness, art, and movement to build coping skills. Facilitators weave in positive psychology and somatic practices.

  • Weekly youth circles segmented by age group
  • Creative arts labs co-led by teaching artists
  • Digital library of guided meditations and grounding tools

Ideal for

Youth ages 10–18 seeking community, stress relief, and skill-building.

Session cadence

Eight-week series with optional alumni drop-ins.

Caregiver collaboratives

Parent and guardian cohorts share strategies, practice co-regulation techniques, and co-create supportive routines at home.

  • Live coaching with psychologists and social workers
  • Resource swaps and mutual aid connections
  • Family agreements toolkit with conversation prompts

Ideal for

Adults who want to model emotional literacy and strengthen relationships.

Session cadence

Monthly gatherings with optional peer pods between sessions.

For schools

Partnerships that bring Project Smile to campus.

Wellness residencies

Multi-week residencies embed psychologists and teaching artists in classrooms. Together we create calm corners, restorative circles, and faculty training tailored to each school community.

Professional learning

Customized workshops equip educators with trauma-informed practices, crisis response tools, and referral pathways.

  • Climate assessments and action planning
  • Coaching for school counselors and social workers
  • Family engagement nights focused on mental health
